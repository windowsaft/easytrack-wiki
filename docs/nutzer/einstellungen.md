# Settings

**Settings** live under **Profile → Settings** and hold the app-wide preferences. Your goals
and body data are elsewhere — under [Goals](ziele.md) — so this screen stays about *how the
app behaves*, not your targets.

## Language

EasyTrack ships in **English and German**. Pick a fixed language, or **System default** to
follow your phone. The change applies immediately, no restart.

## Units & display

- **Units** — the measurement system (metric).
- **Theme** — the app's visual theme.

## Activity

- **Activity increases budget** — when on (the default), manually logged burned calories are
  added to your daily budget. Turn it off to track activity without letting it change your
  goal. See [Diary](tagebuch.md).

## Product data

Download an **Open Food Facts** pack to extend offline [search](suche-barcode.md) with branded
and barcoded products:

- **Region** — which pack to fetch.
- **Product database** — the sized **Download** / **Update** button with a progress bar; you
  can cancel a running download. The pack is verified and swapped in atomically, so a failed
  download never touches the pack you already have.
- **Load package from file** — import a pack from a local zip instead of downloading.

The pack is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup (see [Privacy](datenschutz.md)).

## Backup

- **Back up data** — export everything as a zip you own.
- **Restore data** — import a backup zip.

Both are covered in detail under [Backup](datensicherung.md).

## Diagnostics

- **Export log** — share the app log as a file, handy when reporting a problem.
- **Clear log** — discard the current log entries.

## Updates

For sideloaded installs, EasyTrack checks the releases page and shows a **New version
available** banner at the top of Settings when there's a newer build. See
[Installation](installation.md#updating).
